<p>Every person's journey is shaped by the people who walk alongside them. A parent who sacrifices so their child can dream bigger. A teacher who sees potential before achievement appears. <h2>Relationships are our greatest investment</h2>
<p>Many people understand financial investments. We save, we plan, we diversify, we think about long-term growth. Yet we often underestimate the importance of investing in relationships. <p>Every conversation, every act of service, every moment of listening becomes an investment in relational wealth — creating opportunities money alone cannot purchase: a sense of belonging, a feeling of purpose, a source of resilience, a foundation of trust. Emotional resilience helps us navigate life's challenges; financial resilience helps us create stability and opportunity. Relationships connect the two by providing the support, wisdom, and collaboration necessary to thrive.</p>

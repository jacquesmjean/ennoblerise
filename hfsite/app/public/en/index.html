Rise with us

Beyond empowering — ennobling

Empowerment assumes power must be given. Ennoblement knows it is already there.

Too often, society extracts the natural strength, creativity, and dignity within people — only to return fragments of that power through programs and opportunities. Ennobling reverses the cycle.

The role of education, mentorship, and leadership is not to distribute power, but to awaken and unleash it. When people rise grounded in kindness, compassion, humility, grace, and purposeful impact, they do more than succeed. They elevate families, strengthen communities, and inspire collective progress.

“The goal is not simply empowerment. It is cultivating humane leaders whose awakened spirit transforms the world.”

— Dr. Kasthuri Henry, Founder & Executive Director

Our Mission

To ennoble youth, women, and educators across the world by nurturing emotional resilience, financial independence, and mindful leadership. Through education, mentorship, and sustainable initiatives, EnnobleRise Global Trust builds compassionate leaders who rise to create inclusive communities grounded in dignity, peace, and collective prosperity.

Our Vision

A humane and mindful world where resilient communities cultivate purpose-driven leaders who champion kindness, social justice, and environmental stewardship — fostering lasting peace and shared prosperity for all.

“Building to Last and Ennobling for Success”

From adversity to purpose

Meet Dr. Kasthuri Henry

Founder & Executive Director

Every movement begins with a story. The story behind EnnobleRise Global Trust begins not in a boardroom, but in a childhood shaped by civil war, resilience, and an unwavering belief in the dignity of the human spirit.

Dr. Kas Henry's journey — chronicled in her international bestselling book, Ennobled for Success: From Civil War to a US CFO — traces a path from adversity to global leadership as a financial strategist, professor, and corporate executive. The most profound lessons did not come from spreadsheets or board meetings. They came from people: teachers who shaped young minds, women who carried families through hardship, and youth whose potential waited only for opportunity.
